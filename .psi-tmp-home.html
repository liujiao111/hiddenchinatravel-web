First-trip prep

6 steps to prepare your independent China trip

    Check your visa path

    Select your passport country for a quick visa-free signal — then open the full checker for stay length and transit rules.

  • Step 2

    Get online before you land

    Data + a VPN backup so maps, messages, and payments still work on day one.

  • Step 3

    Set up payments

    Finish wallet setup at home — many shops expect a QR code, not a foreign card alone.

  • Step 4

    Install maps that work

    Google Maps is unreliable on the mainland — learn Amap before you need an address in Chinese.
