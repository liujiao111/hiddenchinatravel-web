# Dali SEO Content & Conversion Playbook

> Status: approved content direction. Use this playbook for all future Dali articles.
>
> Audience: English-speaking foreign visitors planning independent travel in China. Assume they do not speak Chinese, have no local phone number or bank card, and need practical routes—not a group tour.

## 1. Purpose

Build Dali as Hidden China Travel's first city-level SEO cluster while preserving the site's core role: a local partner for independent China travel.

The content should not imitate a destination listicle. It should help travelers make a real decision, prepare for China-specific systems, and move naturally toward either:

1. a useful free next step (Survival Kit / practical guide);
2. an execution action (a relevant, disclosed train, flight, or hotel affiliate link); or
3. custom route planning when the traveler's route is still genuinely unclear.

**Service revenue is primary.** Hotel, train, and flight affiliate links are secondary and must not influence recommendations.

## 2. Existing site context and hard IA rules

- `/china-destinations/yunnan` is already the Yunnan regional pillar and the worked Kunming–Dali–Lijiang 7-day loop.
- `/china-itinerary-planning` and `/how-to-plan-china-itinerary` are the China-wide planning layer.
- Dali articles use flat root-level article URLs (for example, `/dali-travel-guide`), consistent with the existing article model.
- Do **not** create `/china/yunnan/dali/...`, `/china-destinations/dali`, `/yunnan-itinerary`, or `/yunnan-travel-guide`.
- Do **not** treat a planned URL as published. Only link to it after it exists.
- All internal links use relative URLs and open in a new tab.

## 3. Cluster scope and anti-cannibalisation

Use one substantial page per search intent. Do not create thin articles merely because a keyword has a different modifier.

| Do not split into separate pages | One page that owns the intent |
|---|---|
| Dali itinerary 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 days | `/dali-itinerary` |
| Dali weather January through December | `/best-time-to-visit-dali` |
| Dali Ancient Town things to do / night / walking / food | `/dali-ancient-town` |
| Kunming to Dali / Lijiang to Dali / airport transfer | `/how-to-get-to-dali` |
| Dali Ancient Town vs Erhai | H2 in `/where-to-stay-in-dali` |
| Xizhou / Shuanglang / Cangshan guides in Phase 1 | H2 sections in Erhai, Things to Do, or Hidden Gems |
| Yunnan itinerary / Yunnan guide | Existing `/china-destinations/yunnan` |

The Dali pillar should give a direct answer and route readers to specialist pages. It must not write every specialist section so fully that it competes with its own cluster.

## 6. Conversion system

### Conversion principle

Do not make every reader buy a custom itinerary. A traveler who only wants to know whether Dali Old Town is worth visiting is not necessarily ready to pay for a route.

Choose the CTA based on the reader's current decision:

| Reader state | Page examples | Primary conversion | Secondary conversion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exploring a destination | Travel Guide, Things to Do, Hidden Gems | Continue to the best next guide / Survival Kit | Planner, lightly presented |
| Choosing cities, days, or pace | Itinerary, Dali vs Lijiang | Yunnan-specific itinerary planner | Yunnan regional page |
| Selecting an overnight base | Where to Stay | Hotel search with disclosure | Planner for unresolved area/route trade-offs |
| Ready to book travel | How to Get to Dali | Relevant train or flight search with disclosure | Train booking / Didi practical guide |
| Solving logistics on the ground | Erhai, Without a Car | Survival Kit and practical system guides | Planner for a route that remains unresolved |
| Filling in an existing route | Ancient Town, Food, Best Time | Relevant next article or save/prepare action | Planner light entry |

### Required CTA types

#### A. Free preparation CTA

Use on every Dali page when the traveler needs China travel systems, especially on transport, car-free, itinerary, and first-visit pages.

**Copy direction**

> Before you lock Dali hotels: set up data, payments, maps, and train-booking access.

Link to `/survival-kit`.

Contextual support links, used only where relevant:

- payments: `/payments-in-china`, `/alipay-for-foreigners-china`
- data: `/internet-in-china`, `/best-esim-for-china-travel`
- maps: `/maps-navigation-in-china`
- local rides: `/how-to-use-didi-china-foreigners`
- trains: `/transport-in-china`, `/book-china-high-speed-rail-foreigners`
- passport hotels: `/hotels-in-china`

#### B. Service CTA: route decision

Use prominently only on pages where the user is deciding their city order, number of nights, accommodation base, or independent-travel shape:

- `/dali-itinerary`
- `/dali-vs-lijiang`
- `/where-to-stay-in-dali`
- `/dali-without-a-car`
- the Dali pillar, but less prominently

**Copy direction**

> Not sure whether to spend two nights in Dali or continue to Lijiang? Tell us your dates and travel pace. We’ll build an independent Yunnan route you can follow yourself.

Link to `/china-itinerary-planner?dest=yunnan&shape=loop-7#plan-trip`.

Where a visitor needs to understand the product before acting, link to `/services/custom-itinerary`.

Do not promise a guided tour, a booking package, or on-the-ground accompaniment. The service is a 1-to-1 planning PDF and local-partner advice for independent travel.

#### C. Affiliate execution CTA

Only use after the page has done the decision-making work. An affiliate link is an execution tool, not the article's main point.

| Page | Appropriate link |
|---|---|
| How to Get to Dali | `/go/trip-trains`, `/go/trip-flight` |
| Where to Stay in Dali | `/go/trip-hotels` |
| Relevant attraction discussion | `/go/trip-tickets` |

Place a clear disclosure near the first affiliate recommendation:

> This article contains affiliate links. If you book or buy through them, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Never recommend a hotel, route, or ticket because it pays commission. Paid planning must remain independent of accommodation and OTA commissions.

### CTA placement

Use fewer, better-timed prompts:

1. **In context:** immediately after an actionable decision (for example, after the accommodation-area table or a train-transfer explanation).
2. **Near the end:** one primary CTA matching the article intent.
3. **Related next steps:** no more than two relevant internal links.

Avoid a generic “Plan my China trip” banner after every H2. It weakens trust and turns practical editorial content into a sales page.

## 8. Editorial standards

- Answer the practical question early.
- Be current, specific, direct, and honest.
- Write for independent foreign visitors, not domestic social-media traffic.
- Include practical China-specific context: passport hotel check-in, payment backups, eSIM/data, Chinese map pins, DiDi, trains, and booking friction where useful.
- Do not use tourism-board language or empty adjectives such as “ultimate,” “amazing,” “magical,” or “everything you need to know.”
- Do not treat a group-tour schedule as the default itinerary. Say what is worth skipping, what is crowded, and what requires more time.
- Do not fabricate reviews, lived experience, current schedules, prices, or “hidden” places.
- Use affiliate links sparingly, with disclosure, and only when the action matches the article.
